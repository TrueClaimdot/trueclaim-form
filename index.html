Claimant Registration & Disbursement Authorization

Complete all required fields below. This form registers your claim and authorizes electronic disbursement of your approved funds.

Important — Disbursement Notice

Electronic delivery only. Approved claim funds are disbursed directly to a financial institution account held in the claimant's name. No checks are issued or mailed, so no mailing or courier charges of any kind apply.

No fees, ever. Claimants are never asked to pay anything to register, verify, or receive their claim.

Processing time. Estimated 1–3 business days following completed verification. Applicable taxes are settled in accordance with the claim documentation, as stated in your notification letter.

What happens next — step by step:
  1. Transmission. Your registration is sent securely to our office and placed in your claim file under the submission reference above.
  2. Verification (same day to 1 business day). Your case manager reviews every detail against the claim documentation on file. If anything needs attention — a mistyped account number or a name that does not match — we will contact you at the phone number or email you provided.
  3. Scheduling. Once verification is complete, your disbursement is scheduled for electronic release to the account you authorized — estimated 1–3 business days.
  4. Completion. You receive a final confirmation from your case manager when the release is done.

Why we might call you: only to verify your identity, confirm your account details, or correct a small mismatch. If you miss our call, do not worry — we always follow up in writing, and your file is never cancelled because of a missed call.

For your protection, please remember: our office will never ask you for an upfront payment, a gift card, or your online banking password. Anyone who asks for those is not from our office.

Keep your submission reference number and quote it in any correspondence with our office.

Questions, or need to correct something you submitted? Email us at or call or text — Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CT. Please quote your submission reference.
